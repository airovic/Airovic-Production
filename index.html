Questions, answered.

Is Vidqora free?

Yes. You can paste a link and download without paying or creating an account.

Which qualities can I get?

You see every quality the source offers, from 360p up to 1080p or 4K, plus MP3 audio.

Can I download private videos?

No. Only public links work. Posts that need a login or belong to a private account cannot be fetched.

Where does the file go?

To your browser's Downloads folder on a computer, or to Files or Gallery on your phone.

Is it okay to download any video?

Only save content you own or have permission to use. Respect creators' rights and each platform's terms of service.

Do I need an app?

No. Vidqora runs in your browser on any device.
